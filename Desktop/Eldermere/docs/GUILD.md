# The Adventurers' Guild — ranks and crest

Status: design reference (25-Oct). The crest system is planned, not built.

## The crest

A heart wrapped in a golden dragon holding a crystalline spear. Members wear it to show their ranks — each part of the
crest shows a different one:

| Part | Ranking |
|---|---|
| **The background** | **Overall rank** — the average of the other three |
| **The heart** | **Humanitarian** — missions that benefit society without being strictly combat |
| **The spear** | **Combat for the guild and Eldermere** — fighting in their name (separate from hunting) |
| **The dragon** | **Monster hunting** — sheer combat prowess, independent of guild contribution |

## The ranks

| Rank | Who | What it gets you |
|---|---|---|
| **Black** | Unranked — or secret, or unknown | — |
| **Wooden** | Anyone who walks in and asks to join | Last for any job, lowest priority for every guild service |
| **Bronze** | A few missions done, or a common monster brought in as proof | The most common rank: normal access to training areas and services |
| **Silver** | A task that greatly benefits the guild or Eldermere, or a high-tier monster | Special access to training fields and instructors; high priority for all services |
| **Gold** | Services that would otherwise have cost many lives or much property; lesser drakes, greater yetis and other formidable beasts accepted | Absolute access to all services and trainers, free meals and rooms, the artifact hall |
| **Crystal** | Exploits that benefited the whole world, or felling god-equivalents | Unparalleled treatment; humans, with their short lives, rarely earn it |

Monsters are measured against the same ranks (docs/MONSTERS.md: hazard scale and guild-rank equivalent), which is
how a monster brought in counts toward the dragon.

## Planned (the crest system)

Four rank tracks on the character, each fed by its own deeds (humanitarian jobs, guild combat, monster kills by
rank); the overall rank the average; the crest drawn from the four; what each rank opens in the guild (training,
instructors, rooms, the artifact hall, job priority).
