Swarm rules: up to 20 attackers at low altitude, 20 circling above them, and up to 60 visible reserve bats in the high band. Only the attacking band swoops. The remaining logical members are held in reserve and aren't rendered.

For a fair comparison, keep the population, camera distance and speed the same, let each mode settle, then compare draw calls and CPU frame time. GPU timings aren't exposed by this simple browser test.
