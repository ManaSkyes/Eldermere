# The world of Eldermere — quick reference

Notes for building the game, gathered from the book and the author's world-building journals.
More side stories and journal material will be added over time.

## Geography

- **Eldermere** — the city at the center of the world and the center of all trade on the continent.
  It sits where one of the cataclysms struck: the asteroid's remnants formed the mountain the city
  stands on. A 20 m gate guarded by two emerald-eyed golems.
- **The flatlands** — over the years the land beside the mountain evened out into wide, flat country.
- **Windsong Canyon** — the harder rock around the edge of the impact site became a great ring of
  canyons and mountains that surrounds Eldermere. (Book chapter 4.)

## History

- **The cataclysms** came to the world in the distant past. One impact site is where Eldermere now
  stands.
